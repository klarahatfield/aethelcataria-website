Herbalist Cat's Secret Garden Universe

Aethel Cataria

Old magic, grown slow, under a patient moon.

A bespectacled, ginger-furred cat alchemist named Baba tends a garden that has kept its own time for three hundred years — by the moon, not the clock. He is waiting for you, the way old things wait: unhurried, and in no rush to tell you everything at once. This is a dark, cosy fantasy about staying somewhere long enough to become part of it.

The Author

The One Keeping the Record

A world that began as illustrations is now, slowly, becoming words as well

Klara Hatfield

I write under the name Klara Hatfield. This is where I keep an account of the garden, and of the ones who live in it.

The whole world began as a handful of illustrations, before it grew too large for pictures alone to hold. The Herbalist Cat's Secret Garden is the first book to grow out of it.

Before anything else, I try to get the small things right — three young paws mishandling a mortar and pestle, the particular hush of mist over a lagoon at dawn, the quiet, patient tick of a heart kept warm in a drawer. The story always comes after.

This book doesn't end the garden's story. Somewhere beneath the western woods, a few more are still waiting their turn.
